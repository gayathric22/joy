B2B Customization

OEM / ODM Manufacturing Workflow

How Sree Garments & JOY turn tech packs, drawings, and custom uniform requirements into ready-to-ship export orders.

1

Tech Pack & Specs Review

Submit specifications, measurements, fabric weight preferences, and target quantities. Feasibility reviewed within 24 hours.

2

Fabric Sourcing & Patterning

Sourcing Head Mr. M. Aravinth secures samples while Production Manager Mr. R. Pradap develops fit and shrinkage-calibrated patterns.

3

Pre-Production Sampling

A physical pre-production sample (PPS) is stitched and dispatched for buyer approval before bulk cutting.

4

Bulk Cut-to-Pack Run

On sign-off, full production runs across 23 machines with inline inspection and steam-press packaging.

Scope of Customization

Pattern & Grading Development

Custom size grading from XS to 5XL for domestic or international size standards.

Fabric GSM & Dye Matching

Knitted 140–320 GSM cottons, pique, fleece, woven twills, poplin, and custom lab-dip Pantone matching.

Branding & Trim Customization

Woven main labels, care labels, hangtags, custom buttons, zippers, and poly-bag packaging.

Custom Add-ons

Computerized Tajima embroidery, high-density crests, monograms, and silk monograms for VIP orders.

Zero-Defect Standards

Quality Assurance System

How we maintain strict dimensional tolerances, stitch density control, and finish standards across basic and hi-fashion runs. ISO 9001:2015 certified quality.

01

Inward Fabric Audit

Every fabric roll undergoes four-point inspection for GSM uniformity, shade variance, yarn slubs, and shrinkage tolerance before cutting.

02

In-Line Stitching QA

Inline checkers monitor stitches per inch (SPI), seam elasticity, and thread tension at each of the 23 sewing stations.

03

Final Cut-to-Pack Audit

Finished garments are 100% thread-trimmed, steam-pressed, measured against tech packs, and carton-sealed under QC supervision.

Fabric Testing

Durability Benchmarks

Standard Test Cycles

  • 50+ industrial wash cycles tested per batch
  • Colour-fastness and shrinkage audits before approval
  • Stitch density and seam strength checks at every station
  • Antimicrobial and flame-retardant fabric verification where specified
